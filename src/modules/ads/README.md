# Zynocs AdTech Operations (`ads.zynocs.com`)

## Target Audience
- Internal Staff Only: Ad Operations Team, Monetization Managers, Leadership

## Primary Functionality
- Internal ad inventory management across Zynocs products
- Ad unit placements, floor prices, and auction controls
- Ad creative review & approval queue
- Fraud filtering & invalid traffic (IVT) detection
- Fill rate & network revenue monitoring

## Architecture Notes
- High-security internal dashboard.
- Connects directly to Ad Server & RTB (Real-Time Bidding) microservices.
